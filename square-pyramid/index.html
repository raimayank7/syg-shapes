Square pyramid

A square pyramid has a square base and four triangular faces that meet at a point above the centre of the base. The pyramids of Egypt have this shape.

Formulas

  • Slant height l = √(h2 + a2 ⁄ 4)
  • Volume = ⅓ a2h
  • Lateral surface area = 2al
  • Total surface area = a2 + 2al

a = base side and h = height.

Open the calculator

Properties

  • 5 faces, 8 edges and 5 corners.
  • The four side faces are equal isosceles triangles.
  • The height runs from the top to the centre of the base. The slant height runs down the middle of a side face.

Why the formulas work

Any pyramid has one third of the volume of the prism with the same base and height, just as a cone is one third of its cylinder. Here the prism is a box of volume a²h, so the pyramid is ⅓a²h. Each side face is a triangle with base a and height l, so the four together have area 4 × ½al = 2al.

Worked example

Take a square pyramid with base side 10 cm and height 12 cm.

  1. Slant height. Slant height l = √(h2 + a2 ⁄ 4). Here: √(12² + 10² ⁄ 4) = 13 cm.
  2. Volume. Volume = ⅓ a2h. Here: ⅓ × 10 × 10 × 12 = 400 cm³.
  3. Lateral surface area. Lateral surface area = 2al. Here: 2 × 10 × 13 = 260 cm².
  4. Total surface area. Total surface area = a2 + 2al. Here: 10² + 2 × 10 × 13 = 360 cm².

Finding a missing measurement

From the slant height, h = √(l² − a² ⁄ 4): a base side of 10 cm and a slant height of 13 cm give h = √(169 − 25) = 12 cm. From the volume, h = 3V ÷ a²: a volume of 400 cm³ on that base gives 1200 ÷ 100 = 12 cm.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using the vertical height for the area of the side faces. They need the slant height.
  • Forgetting the one third in the volume.

Where you see it

The pyramids of Giza, some tents, the roofs of towers and gazebos, and certain paperweights are square pyramids.

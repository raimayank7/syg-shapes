Equilateral triangle

An equilateral triangle has three equal sides. Its three angles are also equal, each 60°. It is the regular polygon with the fewest sides.

Formulas

  • Area = (√3 ⁄ 4) a2
  • Perimeter = 3a
  • Height = (√3 ⁄ 2) a

a = side.

Open the calculator

Properties

  • All sides are equal and every angle is 60°.
  • It has three lines of symmetry.
  • Each height is also a median and an angle bisector, and all three meet at one point.
  • Six equilateral triangles fit together to make a regular hexagon.

Why the formulas work

Drop a height from the top corner. It cuts the base in half and makes two right-angled triangles with hypotenuse a and base a/2. By Pythagoras the height is the root of a² minus a²/4, which is (√3/2)a. Half of base times height then gives (√3/4)a². The value of √3 is about 1.732.

Worked example

Take a equilateral triangle with side 10 cm.

  1. Area. Area = (√3 ⁄ 4) a2. Here: (√3 ⁄ 4) × 10 × 10 = 43.3 cm².
  2. Perimeter. Perimeter = 3a. Here: 3 × 10 = 30 cm.
  3. Height. Height = (√3 ⁄ 2) a. Here: (√3 ⁄ 2) × 10 = 8.66 cm.

Finding a missing measurement

From the perimeter, divide by 3. From the height, the side is 2h ÷ √3. From the area, the side is √(4A ÷ √3): an area of about 43.3 cm² gives a side of 10 cm, since (√3 ⁄ 4) × 100 is about 43.3.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using ½ × a × a for the area. The height is not a, it is about 0.866 times a.
  • Rounding √3 to 1.7 too early. Use 1.732 and round at the end.

Where you see it

Road warning signs, the rack of balls in pool and snooker, some trusses and the faces of a tetrahedron are equilateral triangles.

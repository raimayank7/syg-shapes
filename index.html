How to learn mensuration

Do not start by memorising the formula sheet. Start with the square and the rectangle, where area can be counted in unit squares, and see every other formula grow out of them. Draw the figure for every question and write the measurements on it. Decide whether the question is about an edge, a covering or a filling before you choose a formula. And write the unit after every answer.

Common questions

What is the difference between area and perimeter?

Perimeter is the length of the boundary of a flat shape, measured in units such as cm or m. Area is the surface inside it, measured in square units such as cm² or m².

What is the difference between surface area and volume?

Surface area is the total area of the outside of a solid, in square units. Volume is the space it fills, in cubic units.

Should I use 22/7 or 3.14 for π?

Use the value your question gives. If it gives none, 22/7 is usual in Indian school books, especially when the radius is a multiple of 7.

How many litres is one cubic metre?

One cubic metre is 1000 litres. One litre is 1000 cubic centimetres.

Regular polygon

A regular polygon has all its sides equal and all its angles equal. The equilateral triangle and the square are the first two. Then come the regular pentagon (5 sides), hexagon (6), heptagon (7), octagon (8), nonagon (9) and decagon (10).

Formulas

  • Area = na2 ⁄ (4 tan(180° ⁄ n))
  • Perimeter = na
  • Interior angle = (n − 2) × 180° ⁄ n
  • Apothem = a ⁄ (2 tan(180° ⁄ n))

n = number of sides and a = side.

Open the calculator

Properties

  • The interior angles add up to (n − 2) × 180°.
  • Each exterior angle is 360° ÷ n.
  • It has n lines of symmetry.
  • All its corners lie on one circle.
  • The apothem is the distance from the centre to the middle of a side.

Why the formulas work

Join the centre to every corner. The polygon splits into n equal triangles, each with base a and height equal to the apothem. Its area is therefore n × ½ × a × apothem, or half the perimeter times the apothem. The apothem comes from the right-angled triangle formed by half a side and the angle 180°/n at the centre.

Worked example

Take a regular polygon with number of sides 6 and side 10 cm.

  1. Area. Area = na2 ⁄ (4 tan(180° ⁄ n)). Here: 6 × 10² ⁄ (4 × tan(180° ⁄ 6)) = 259.81 cm².
  2. Perimeter. Perimeter = na. Here: 6 × 10 = 60 cm.
  3. Each interior angle (degrees). Interior angle = (n − 2) × 180° ⁄ n. Here: (6 − 2) × 180 ⁄ 6 = 120.
  4. Apothem. Apothem = a ⁄ (2 tan(180° ⁄ n)). Here: 10 ⁄ (2 × tan(180° ⁄ 6)) = 8.66 cm.

Finding a missing measurement

From the interior angle, find the number of sides through the exterior angle. An interior angle of 135° means an exterior angle of 45°, and 360 ÷ 45 = 8 sides: an octagon. From the perimeter, the side is the perimeter ÷ n.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using the formula for a polygon whose sides are not all equal. It works only for regular polygons.
  • Mixing up interior and exterior angles. For a hexagon they are 120° and 60°.

Where you see it

Honeycomb cells and many floor tiles are regular hexagons, a STOP sign is a regular octagon, and old 2 rupee coins in India had eleven sides.
